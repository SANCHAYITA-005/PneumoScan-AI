Understanding the X-Ray

General educational comparison of chest X-ray appearances.

N

Normal Appearance

  • Generally clearer lung fields.
  • No obvious focal air-space opacity.
  • Relatively uniform appearance.
P

Pneumonia-associated Appearance

  • Possible patchy white opacities.
  • Areas of increased lung density.
  • Possible infiltrates or consolidation.
